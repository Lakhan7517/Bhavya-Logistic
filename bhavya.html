ABOUT BHAVYA LOGISTIC

Connecting Businesses Across India

Bhavya Logistic provides a comprehensive range of services, from freight management to customs clearance and advisory services. With our head office in Kolhapur and Pune, along with offices in Gujarat, we are positioned to serve our clients efficiently.

We also operate warehouses in Kolhapur and Gujarat, enabling us to support the complete logistics and supply chain requirements of our clients.

Our approach seamlessly integrates road transportation, contract logistics, freight forwarding and customs advisory services to deliver versatile, end-to-end, door-to-door multimodal supply chain solutions.
